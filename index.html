Miss Blue

Scientific Name:

Miss Blue

Discovered by / at:

Camille, 10/05/2026

Family:

White Manta

Habitat:

Deep ocean, but "dream" sometimes

Appearance:

Blue has a distinctive blue coloration and a large, flat body. She has a long tail and powerful fins that help her navigate the deep ocean. A cute smiling face will appear when she is rotating.

Key Characteristics:

  • Swimming with families.
  • Rotating and creating bubbles.
  • Dreaming and playing with particles.

What we know so far about Blue? "Blue" is a unique manta ray, distinguished by her unusual color. She lives in the deep sea, staying close to her family. Her favorite thing to do is spin her body and blow bubbles. Sometimes, she drifts into a deep sleep, entering a dream world where she plays with tiny, floating particles. But soon, she returns to reality, continuing to swim alongside her family.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature "Blue" always follow the pace of mouse. If you press the mouse, she will rotate and blow bubbles. If you move the mouse, she will follow the mouse. To enter the dream world, simply move the mouse to the blue circle in the screen. Then you will find yourself in a world of particles, where Blue can play with them.
